▲ tip = the fill — its apex sits on the bar and price the trade actually opened at, which is the next bar's open after the signal bar closed, plus slippage. ✕ = the exit. Colour is win/loss, not buy/sell. Dots on the gold line = this timeframe's own UT cross; the rail below shows every timeframe's UT flips on one axis, which is where confluence becomes visible. A hollow last candle is the forming bar — drawn live, never used for a signal. Hollow chevrons are your own fills imported from the broker report — up = buy, down = sell, outlined green if that position made money; the small circle is where it closed. The rail's signal lane is the strategy's own entries (solid) and exits (hollow), and mine is yours, so agreement and disagreement are one glance apart. Hover a rail lane to lift it out of the stack and push the rest back, and to read that channel's win rate over 7 / 30 / 90 days against its own break-even — a mined pattern that has stopped working shows up as a falling sequence there and nowhere else. Click the lane to pin it; click again to release. MACD is not in the app — it is a backtest-only addition. · Drag to pan, scroll to zoom, Shift+drag to measure, Esc clears it. The crosshair follows your mouse and prints the price on the axis and the time under it; Details turns on the full hover card, which is off by default because it covered the price you were reading. There is empty room to the right of the newest bar so the live edge is never pinned against the axis. Time switches the axis between Malaysia, New York and UTC; the tooltip always shows all three, and New York follows EDT/EST rather than a fixed offset. Markers land on the bar containing the fill, so on a 4H chart several 1H trades can share one bar.
